BookStore GUI App

Image Credit: Jennifer Wuestling | artstation.com/izaskun

Greetings,

I am Hamza Hashmi, and I am excited to present an overview of another one of my side projects — the BookStore GUI App. The aim of this side project analyze, design and implement a bookstore application using state design pattern.

The aplication is graphical user interface (GUI) based and is developed using JavaFX. All the relevent data in the app is written to 2 files book.txt and customer.txt. Whenever the [x] is pressed, the old data is overwritten by the current data.

The login screen has a username field, password filed and a login button. There are 2 types of users to this application, the Owner and the Customer. The Owners username is admin and password is admin.


The Owner-start-screen has 3 functionalities. 'Books' which allows the owner to add books and their preices to the book catalog. 'Customers' which allows the onwer to add Customers along with data in relation to their username, password and points. Each cutsomer has a certain amount of points they obtain by buying books and when a certain amount of points has been accuired they are able to get discounts on their next book purchase.'Logout' which returns to the login screen.


The Customer-start-screen has just 1 functinality which is to purchase book. At the top part of the screem, the customers total points will be displayed. In the middle of the screen is the book catalog where the books name, price and a check box for each book are located. And at the bottom of the screen is has the buy, redeem points and buy and logout button.


When the customer clicks either the buy button or redeem and buy button, a new screen opens up which is the Customer-cost-screen. This screen has the total cost of all the items checked off, the current points they have and a log out button.
